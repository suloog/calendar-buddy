# [SRC-14] Analyst notes — product owner's request 2026-11-10, the menu bar icon's click, reminders on invitations and notifications of invitations

## Summary

The product owner asked for one change and two new things: a click on the menu bar icon should take them to the app, with the menu on a right click; on events they do not own and cannot edit they should still be able to change their own reminders, which in Google are each guest's own; and they want notifications of invitations. Asked about the open choices, they chose to change the reminders in the event's details, buttons to answer on the invitation's notification, and to be told at the next start about invitations that came while the app was off.

## Items

- [SRC-14-01] "kliknięcie na ikonę w status barze powinno przechodzić do aplikacji; ewentualnie jeśli się da to na prawe kliknięcie myszą powinno się pojawiać menu" — a click on the menu bar icon opens the app; a right click shows the menu. — *conversation 2026-11-10*
- [SRC-14-02] "w wydarzeniach których nie jestem właścicielem i nie mogę edytować powinienem móc zmieniać powiadomienia (one są zawsze moje z tego co pamiętam i zmieniają się tylko dla mnie)" — on events the user cannot change, they can still change their own reminders, which change only for them. — *conversation 2026-11-10*
- [SRC-14-03] "powiadomienia o zaproszeniach" — a notification when an invitation comes. — *conversation 2026-11-10*
- [SRC-14-04] Asked where the reminders of such an event are changed, the product owner chose the event's details, with the editor's reminder rows and a Save, over an editor with every other field locked; told that Google refuses any change on a calendar the user may only read, they kept it to invitations. — *conversation 2026-11-10*
- [SRC-14-05] Asked which buttons a notification of an invitation has, the product owner chose Accept, Maybe and Decline, a click opening the invitation in the app. — *conversation 2026-11-10*
- [SRC-14-06] Asked about invitations that came while the app was off, the product owner chose that the app remembers which invitations it told about and tells about the new ones at its next start; the first synchronization of a newly connected account does not tell about all its invitations. — *conversation 2026-11-10*
