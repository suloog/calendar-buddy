# [SRC-13] Analyst notes — product owner's request 2026-11-05, the menu bar and notifications on macOS

## Summary

With the first stage of the app on macOS installed (`SRC-12`), the product owner asked to start the improvements on macOS on a new branch: above all, closing the window should leave an icon in the menu bar, and notifications should have their buttons and open their event when clicked. Asked about the two open choices, they chose that the app leaves the Dock while its window is closed, and a monochrome menu bar icon.

## Items

- [SRC-13-01] "dobra to teraz zacznijmy na nowym branchu kilka usprawnień w MacOS - przedewszystkim żeby dało się zamknąć okno i ikonka w menu pojawiała + w powiadomieniu + dało kliknąć na powiadomienie" — closing the window leaves an icon in the menu bar; notifications get their buttons and can be clicked. — *conversation 2026-11-05*
- [SRC-13-02] Asked what happens to the Dock icon while the window is closed, the product owner chose that the app leaves the Dock, with only the menu bar icon left, as menu bar apps do and as the tray does on Linux. — *conversation 2026-11-05*
- [SRC-13-03] Asked how the menu bar icon looks, the product owner chose a monochrome icon, which macOS colours for a light or dark menu bar like the system's own icons, over the app's coloured icon. — *conversation 2026-11-05*
