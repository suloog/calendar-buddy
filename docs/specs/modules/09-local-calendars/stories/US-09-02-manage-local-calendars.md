## Description

A "Local calendars" section in the settings to manage any number of local calendars, which then behave in the side panel, the pickers and the settings as an account's calendars do. The calendars' default notifications, also set in this section, come with `US-09-04`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The settings have a "Local calendars" section listing each local calendar with its colour and name; adding one with a name makes it appear at once under "Local" in the side panel, in the calendar picker and in the settings' list of calendars to disable.
- [ ] Renaming a local calendar and choosing another colour from the palette changes the side panel, the picker and its events at once.
- [ ] A local calendar can be hidden with the side panel's toggle, disabled and enabled again in the settings, and muted from its menu, as a Google calendar can.
- [ ] A local calendar can be chosen as the default calendar in the settings; the quick box then starts in it.
- [ ] Deleting a local calendar with events asks for confirmation, naming the number of events and saying it cannot be undone; after confirming, neither the calendar nor its events are anywhere in the app. An empty local calendar is deleted without a question.
- [ ] Deleting the default local calendar forgets the choice, and new events go where they go when none is chosen: the first account's primary calendar, else the first calendar that can take events (`DEC-104`).
- [ ] With no account connected, deleting the last local calendar shows the welcome screen; with local calendars left, removing the last Google account does not.

## Out of scope / what not to worry about

- Default notifications of a local calendar — `US-09-04`.
- Creating, renaming or deleting Google calendars — `DEC-21`.
- Undoing the deletion of a calendar — the confirmation stands in for it (`MOD-09` *Constraints and edge cases*).

## Technical notes

- The section sits in the settings next to **Accounts** and **Calendars** (`DEC-51`, `DEC-102`); the "Local" group comes last in the **Calendars** list too.
- A local calendar's ID is generated by the app; its colour is stored in the local store, not as a local colour override (`MOD-02-F04` stays for Google calendars).
- Deleting a calendar deletes its events, its hidden/disabled/muted settings and, if it was the default, the default choice.
- Decisions: `DEC-178`, `DEC-179`.
